Biology Cell: The Unit of Life Medium

Mitochondria ko cell ka powerhouse kyun kehte hain?

Ye ek sample hai. Neeche apna doubt likhkar Send dabayein.

SHORT ANSWER

Mitochondria cell ke liye ATP (energy) banata hai, isliye ise cell ka powerhouse kaha jata hai.

Step-by-step Explanation

1
Cell ko energy chahiye
Cell ke har kaam, jaise growth aur transport, ke liye energy ki zarurat hoti hai.
2
Energy ka form: ATP
Cell ki energy currency ATP hai, jo turant use ho sakti hai.
3
ATP ka production
Mitochondria ki inner membrane (cristae) par cellular respiration se ATP banta hai.
FORMULA
Glucose + O2 -> CO2 + H2O + ATP
REAL-LIFE EXAMPLE
Jaise power plant poore sheher ko bijli deta hai, waise mitochondria poori cell ko energy deta hai.
